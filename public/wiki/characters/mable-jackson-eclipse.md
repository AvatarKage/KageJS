Mable Jackson, also known as Eclipse, is a 21-year-old American martial arts and hacking prodigy, associate nurse, and scientist specializing in dragonite research. She is the founder and executive leader of Team Dragonight and possesses fire manipulation abilities from a flamasi crystal. Unlike the other members of Team Dragonight, she was hit by a rare ancient crystal that formed over two-and-a-half-thousand-years-ago, whose power is roughly equal to those of the very first dragonights. Apart from her dragonight powers, Mable is gifted with force manipulation abilities in her left wrist by successfully becoming a Stone Warrior after training for most of her life.

## Early Life and Origins

Mable was born into a family with connections to the [Moon Stone Warriors](/groups/moon-stone-warriors), which got her interested at a very young age in learning more about them and eventually following in their footsteps. She began training to become one with the guidance of her uncle, Mike Randall, and actively practiced for over a decade while gaining skills in hacking and medicine. Her quick adaptability made it easier for her to train and tackle new combat styles.

One day, in 2019, while hanging out with her high-school friends, a catastrophic event occurred where a meteorite crashed into western Dullsville. The meteor's dragonite crystals shot out at them granting all extraordinary abilities. Mable has always been a huge Lunari nerd who had read all her books about dragonite and was no stranger to them. Within days of the meteor impact, Dullsville got raided by the plozonies. This is when Mable rose up anonymously and rained fire down on the invaders, forcing them to retreat. Seeing the hope everyone had in her actions, and inspired by superhero comic books and other real-world individuals, she took the initiative upon herself to put an end to the Plutonian War. She formed a team of dragonights consisting of her friends, known as "Team Dragonight" and soon after the Plutonian War ended.

Not too far into the future, Mable graduated from the Stone Warrior Academy and became a Stone Warrior.

## Personal Life

Mable suffers from separation anxiety but always finds comfort in her best friend, Julia Anderson. In September 2021, a heartbreaking event happened involving her as a Stone Warrior, which captured media attention worldwide.

## Career

Mable completed a 12-week training program in 2021 to become a certified nurse assistant then volunteered as that role for a few months. In early 2024, Mable joined Dullsville’s Skorpion Control as an officer. Alongside that, she also attends dangerous cross-continental missions with other Stone Warriors ordered by their leader, Zeng Martini.
